Frequently asked questions

Is ChatPal free?

Yes. You get 10 free messages every day, plus bonus messages as your friendship grows. For more, you can pick a monthly plan or a message pack paid with Telegram Stars.

Does ChatPal remember me?

Yes. ChatPal remembers your name, your plans and the people you talk about. You can see everything it knows about you, and erase any of it, in the ChatPal app.

Which languages does ChatPal speak?

ChatPal answers in your language: English, French, Spanish, Portuguese, German, Italian, Russian, Arabic, Hindi, Indonesian, Turkish and many more.

Can I add ChatPal to my group or channel?

Yes. Add it as an admin and it posts a daily quiz and a topic of the day on the theme you choose, with points, a leaderboard and a weekly champion.

Is ChatPal a real person?

No. ChatPal is an AI companion powered by Claude. It is a friendly presence to talk to, not a replacement for friends, family or professional help.

Do I need to install anything?

No. ChatPal lives inside Telegram. Open the bot, tap Start and say hi.
